# JGME Manuscript + Cover Letter Template

> **Journal:** *Journal of Graduate Medical Education (JGME)*  
> **Purpose:** Reusable submission template for JGME manuscripts, including a cover letter, title page, manuscript structure, AI-specific disclosure fields, references, graphics/supplements, and a final submission checklist.

---

## 0. Choose the manuscript category before writing

| JGME category | Main text limit | Abstract | Tables/Figures/Boxes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Original Research | ≤2,500 words | ≤250 words | ≤5 |
| Qualitative Original Research | ≤3,500 words | ≤250 words | ≤5 |
| Educational Innovation | ≤2,000 words | ≤250 words | ≤5 |
| Review | ≤3,000 words | ≤250 words | ≤5 |
| Brief Report | ≤1,200 words | ≤250 words | ≤2 |
| Perspective | ≤1,200 words | No abstract | ≤2 |
| On Teaching | ≤1,200 words | No abstract | N/A |
| To the Editor | ≤500 words | No abstract | ≤2 |

**Research-style submissions** (Original Research, Educational Innovation, Brief Report) use a structured abstract and structured manuscript.

**Perspective/On Teaching** submissions do not require an abstract.

# Source Notes

This template is based on the current JGME Manuscript Submission Checklist and the JGME AI Teaching Rounds launch editorial. The journal's checklist specifies title-page elements, double spacing and page numbering, numbered references, graphics/supplement citation, structured research-manuscript elements, study-year reporting, response-rate reporting, IRB/ethics placement, limitations, survey supplements, category limits, and figure requirements.

The AI-specific sections are derived from the AI Teaching Rounds framing: disclosure/transparency, human responsibility, protection of PHI, human verification, attention to equity, preserving trainee reasoning, and small testable interventions. These AI fields should be adapted to the actual study and current journal policy.

The cover-letter organization is a practical submission template rather than a journal-mandated fixed syntax. JGME has specifically identified unexplained word-count excess as a cover-letter problem, so a justification field is included when needed.
